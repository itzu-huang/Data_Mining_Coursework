# Random Forest

## Objective

Practice a tree-ensemble classifier on Bank customer data.

## Dataset

Bank customer data referenced by the exercise; the CSV is not included.

## Methods

RandomForestClassifier with `n_estimators=200` and `max_depth=8`, followed by train/test comparison.

## What I Practiced

Compare a tree ensemble's training and testing performance and consider depth as a model-complexity control.

## Key Observation

The exercise illustrates parameterized Random Forest classification; no general performance claim is made from one data split.
